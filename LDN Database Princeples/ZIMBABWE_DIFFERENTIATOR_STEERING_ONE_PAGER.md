# Zimbabwe — why our national LDN platform is not “another country’s system”

**Audience:** EMA steering, ministers, UNCCD focal point  
**One page — Nov 2026 programme**

---

## What we are building

**The Zimbabwe National LDN Data Platform** — authoritative store for national layers, field evidence, verification, and PRAIS-ready exports — plus the **EMA mobile app** (Android/iOS) as the **only** channel for new field submissions.

This is **not** a dashboard, a PRAIS clone, or a copy of Botswana or Panama. It **complies** with UNCCD/GPG and **composes** proven practices from peer countries.

---

## Global compliance (non-negotiable)

- SDG **15.3.1** / SO1-4 via SO1-1, SO1-2, SO1-3  
- Baseline vs **reporting periods**; **1OAO** where nationally adopted  
- **Tier 1 / 2 / 3** evidence explicit  
- PRAIS 4 metadata (Annex II) on national datasets  
- Verification before **lock** for official tables  

---

## What most countries do not combine (Zimbabwe corpus)

| Capability | Typical Party | Zimbabwe (in formation) |
|------------|---------------|-------------------------|
| National unit grid | Global 300 m or sparse CE plots | **~254k** national LDN polygons + field validation |
| Field evidence | Kobo/sporadic apps | **Governed mobile** + codes + `/api/submit/*` |
| Programme pressures | Often absent in one system | **Mining, invasives, SBC, 47 projects** (EMA layers) |
| Drylands programme | Separate spreadsheets | **ILUP** in same registry as LDN/soil |
| Monitoring network | Ad hoc | **28+** registered sites (growing) |
| Reporting chain | Consultant → PRAIS | **District → national → locked export** (target R3–R5) |

---

## One sentence

> **Zimbabwe links Collect Earth–scale national units, governed mobile evidence, and ministry spatial layers in one verification chain — so PRAIS numbers are defensible to government and UNCCD alike.**
