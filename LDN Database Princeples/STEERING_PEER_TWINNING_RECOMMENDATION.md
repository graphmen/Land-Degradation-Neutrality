## Why these two (from your 11-country list)

### Botswana — “same problem at scale”

- **17,006** Collect Earth plots on a national grid; **1OAO**; national six land-use classes; medium confidence with **ongoing ground truthing**.  
- PRAIS reporting uses **national** data, not defaults only.  
- **Zimbabwe parallel:** **~254k** combined LDN units + **150** intensive validations + mobile app — we are **denser** but need the same **discipline**: grid ↔ field ↔ reporting.

**Adopt:** TWG-style governance (EMA steering = technical working group); plot/unit update cadence.  
**Avoid:** Outsourcing the system of record to FAO Data Studio only — Zimbabwe must host **national corpus** on EMA infrastructure.

### Panama — “platform survives the project”

- **LDN-Panama-WOCAT-FAO** institutionalized through **2030**; basin/sub-basin/**farm** monitoring; **local LDN goal reports** delivered to Ministry of Environment.  
- National land cover **2000–2021**; legend extensions (mangrove, thicket) — participatory national reality vs global 7-class.  
- **Zimbabwe parallel:** We need **ward/basin-level** narrative reports and **ministry handoff**, not only a map.

**Adopt:** Institutionalization checklist (training, socialization, reporting calendar).  
**Avoid:** GEF-project-only tool with no home in EMA ICT after closure.
