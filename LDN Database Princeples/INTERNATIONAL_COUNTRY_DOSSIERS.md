## Dossier — Botswana

**Institution:** Department of Forestry & Range Resources + LDN TWG (FAO DSL-IP).  
**System:** [Botswana Land Degradation Monitoring System](https://bit.ly/botswana_ldn) — national grid, **17,006** Collect Earth plots (4 km densified east).  
**Method:** SO1-1/2/3, **1OAO**, baseline 2000–2015; six national land-use classes.  
**Reporting:** PRAIS with **national** datasets; medium confidence; ongoing ground truthing.  

| Adopt | Adapt | Avoid |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Plot network + systematic CE updates | Scale to **254k** existing ZW polygons + mobile spot checks | Replacing ZW mobile mandate with Collect-only desktop |
| TWG governance | EMA steering = TWG | Outsourcing custodianship to FAO-only tools |

## Zimbabwe positioning (synthesis)

**We are not building:**

- A KPI dashboard  
- A PRAIS clone  
- A single-country copy (Botswana CE-only, Panama basin-only, etc.)

**We are building:**

1. **National data corpus** — your shapefiles + today’s imports + future EMA drops ([`NATIONAL_DATA_CORPUS.md`](./NATIONAL_DATA_CORPUS.md)).  
2. **Governed field evidence** — mobile-only, verification, periods, tiers.  
3. **Analytics & reporting engine** — Trends.Earth/GEE class tools feeding **locked** exports.  
4. **Government platform UX** — Namibia/MEA-class seriousness + Botswana plot discipline + Colombia/Panama ownership.

**Differentiator:** **254k national units** + **operational pressure layers** (mining, invasives, SBC) + **unified drylands/LDN/soil** under one verification chain — most peers split these.
